Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Welcome to BNB BUSINESS Network Bangladesh ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal, financial, and transactional data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related web services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a secure and reliable platform for co-operative savings, safe escrow transactions, and digital remittance, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identity Information: Name, Email Address, Mobile Phone Number, National Identity Card (NID) details/photo, and profile picture.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Co-operative deposits, welfare funds, escrow orders, withdrawal details, and payment histories. This data is handled securely through authenticated live Firestore storage.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect precise or approximate geolocation information to verify the regional compliance of merchant transactions, safe deals, and prevent security breaches/fraudulent accesses.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, browser type, and push notification tokens to ensure critical alerts (such as transaction confirmations or security warnings) are delivered successfully.
  • Camera & Files: Access to your camera and document storage to upload NID verification photographs, transactional receipts, and profile documentation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To authenticate and manage member accounts and verify national identity eligibility.
  • To process cooperative savings, safe escrow deals, and instant peer-to-peer balance transfers.
  • To prevent fraudulent logins, concurrent device abuse, and malicious financial interactions.
  • To send automated notifications, OTP verifications, and real-time ledger updates regarding balance increases or deductions.
  • To comply with regulatory guidelines, financial audits, and national transaction gateway standards.

3. Data Protection and Security

Your data security is our highest priority. We implement robust physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All database communications are governed by secure real-time Firestore rules ensuring only verified, authenticated accounts can read and write data.
  • We enforce industry-standard encryption protocols during data transit and storage.
  • Members can set secure personalized lock-pins within the application to prevent unauthorized local access to transaction and profile screens.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or lease your personal identification or financial information to third parties. We may disclose your information only in the following scenarios:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize the sharing of data for third-party billing, delivery integrations, or bank transfers.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by applicable law, regulation, or legal subpoena to protect against fraud, malicious transactions, or national security threats.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion (অ্যাকাউন্ট ও তথ্য মুছে ফেলার নিয়ম)

We retain your profile data and financial transaction logs only as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill legal, cooperative auditing, and anti-fraud obligations.

How to request account and data deletion:

  • In-App Request: You can submit a deletion or closure request directly from the app profile settings or contact customer support in the app.
  • Web / Email Request: You can send an email to networkbangladeshbnbbusiness@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Phone/Email]".
  • Processing Timeframe: Upon identity confirmation, your personal data, profile photos, and credentials will be permanently erased from active databases within 14 to 30 days, except for financial transaction records that must be retained as mandated by applicable regulatory or statutory accounting laws.

6. Financial Features & Regulatory Disclosure

BNB BUSINESS Network Bangladesh is a cooperative society platform and welfare member fund management system. We do not operate as an open commercial deposit bank or predatory payday lender. All installment plans, welfare funds, and member shares are governed by internal cooperative mutual rules and voluntary mutual savings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services and financial features are strictly intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
