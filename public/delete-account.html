Account & Data Deletion Request

App: BNB BUSINESS Network Bangladesh

Under Google Play's User Data policy and international data protection standards, users have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your account and data through either of the following methods:

  • Option 1 (Email): Send an email directly to networkbangladeshbnbbusiness@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address or providing your registered mobile number and full name.
  • Option 2 (In-App): Navigate to your profile settings in the BNB app, tap Help & Support, and submit an account cancellation request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Personal profile details (Name, Email, Mobile Number, Profile Picture, NID verification copies).
  • Authentication credentials and device identifiers / push notification tokens.
  • Active membership status and preferences.

Retention Exceptions

As a cooperative and financial records platform, certain transaction logs, audited deposit ledgers, or compliance records will be retained solely for statutory accounting, tax, anti-fraud, or legal dispute compliance periods as required by applicable law.

Need Help?

Our team processes deletion requests within 14–30 business days after verifying account ownership.
